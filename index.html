Our big idea

News should make kids say
“Wait, really?!”

Every student deserves something exciting to read. We make a newspaper that turns ten minutes of reading into questions, conversations, and a little more wonder about the world. Keep each issue in your library and come back to your favourite discoveries anytime.

  • Curiosity comes first. Always.
  • Made for real classrooms, not just screens.
  • Free for every school, every month.
Inside every issue

Something to question, solve, make, and share.

?To question

Big questions

Thought-provoking prompts that help kids form opinions, listen well, and explain what they think.

!To understand

News that matters

Clear, age-appropriate stories about the world, with the context that makes headlines easier to understand.

+To try

Life skills lab

Practical activities on cyber safety, money, kindness, communication, and smart choices online.

#To solve

Puzzles and challenges

Brain teasers, mini mysteries, word games and quizzes, made for a satisfying pencil-in-hand break.

&To make

Monthly creative competition

A new chance every month to send in an essay, poem, drawing, or idea of your own.

*To share

Random fun stuff

Strange facts, surprising history, curious creatures, and tiny experiments you will want to tell someone about.

How it works

Getting on board takes minutes.

  1. Register

    Fill out the quick form so we know where the curiosity should go.

  2. We prep each issue

    Our team packs every edition with fresh facts, stories, big questions, skills, puzzles, and ideas.

  3. Curiosity happens

    Students read, wonder, share, create, and see their world in a slightly brighter way.
